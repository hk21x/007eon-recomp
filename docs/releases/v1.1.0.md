An update to the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox, Europe and USA).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.0.0 or later:** open `EoNLauncher.exe`. It offers this release at the top of its window: press **Update the
launcher**. The new launcher shows **A faster way to play**: press **Update**. Your game keeps playing until the new
one is ready, and your saves and settings are kept. Afterwards, **Manage > Remove the old build tools** frees the space
the old build tools took.

## What's new since 1.0.3

### Installing takes a minute, not an hour
- The launcher no longer downloads 1.4 GB of build tools or builds everything from source. It downloads your disc's
  package (the program and a compiler, no game code: one for the European discs, one for the USA disc), and your PC
  builds only the game's code from your disc. How long that takes depends on your PC and connection.
- No Microsoft license step, no Git, no CMake.
- Updates are built beside the game and only replace it once ready: a failed or stopped update leaves the game as it was.

### A new launcher
- Pages for **Game**, **Manage** and **Log**, the steps as a list, and the progress in the footer on every page.
- **Manage** shows the install, opens its folders, frees space, and builds the game again if it will not start.

### Graphics
- **PC Settings** (in the game's Options menus) now has **MSAA** and **Edge smoothing** (Off, FXAA or SMAA) on its
  Graphics page. The README compares them, with pictures.
- New in the settings window (**F1**): **Sharpening**, the scaling filter, enhanced world textures and other options.

### Linux, Steam Deck and Bazzite
- The game's shaders now compile under Wine's shader compiler, where nothing could be drawn before. Wine is not
  supported, but the README's Troubleshooting section has the steps a player used to get it running.

## Files

- **EoNLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **EoN-PAL-package.zip**, **EoN-USA-package.zip** and their **.json**: the packages the launcher downloads, and their
  sizes and checksums. You do not need to download or unzip them.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
