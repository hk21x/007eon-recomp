The first release of the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox): the game's own
code statically recompiled into Windows programs, not an emulator.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game (the European PAL release): an `.iso` of your
disc, or a folder with its files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## Getting started

1. Download **EoNLauncher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own.
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder), accept Microsoft's license for its C++ libraries, press **Build**. The first
   build takes 30 to 90 minutes.
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

## What's in it

- **Widescreen and ultrawide**, on foot and in the driving missions: the view widens to the screen, the HUD and menus
  keep their 16:9 shape in the centre, the sniper scope and the title screen fill the frame.
- **60 FPS driving missions**, a **field of view** setting, **sharp text and HUD** at high resolutions, and the
  console's full 6 channel mix in the driving missions (speech, engine, music).
- **Keyboard and mouse**: direct mouse aiming, the mouse in the menus, on-screen prompts showing your keys, and a
  separate key layout for the driving missions; every key can be rebound.
- **Controllers**: Xbox, PlayStation and Switch, with hot plugging and rumble; modern button pictures.
- **Achievements**: 39, with pop-ups and a list in the settings window.
- **Quality of life**: Quit Game in the main menu, skip the intro logos, hide the HUD for screenshots, a shader cache
  so later sessions run without hitching.

## Known limits

- Only the European (PAL) release is supported; other releases have not been checked.
- Direct3D 12 is the tested renderer.
- The names of achievements for missions after Mardi Gras Mayhem follow the game's text and are still to be checked
  in play.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
