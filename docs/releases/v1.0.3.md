An update of the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox).

**To update:** open `EoNLauncher.exe`. It offers this version, updates itself, then rebuilds the game. Your saves and settings are kept.

## New: settings in the game's own menus

- **PC Settings** is now in **Options** on the main menu and in the pause menu's **Options** tab, so you can change settings with a controller or the keyboard. The first row turns the page; left and right, or a click, change a value. Key bindings, the wheel and the monitor stay in the settings window (**F1**).
- **Language** is a new setting, in PC Settings and in F1, and applies at the next start, in the driving missions too. Each disc has its own languages: English, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Swedish on one European disc, French and German on the other (so German can now be chosen), English on the USA disc. A language your disc does not have is ignored.

## Smoother play

- **Stutter in driving missions.** A fix in 1.0.2 could leave two parts of the game waiting on each other for a tenth of a second, again and again during a mission (seen in Train Chase). They no longer wait. Some slow frames remain while a mission loads.
- **The stutter when button prompts first appear**, for example as you approach a vehicle. The port's button pictures are now drawn in the background. You may see the disc's own pictures for a moment before they change.
- **Smooth slow motion** (new setting, on; Direct3D 12, driving missions at 60 frames a second). Slow motion moves in steps, as on the Xbox, but at 60 frames a second the steps stand out, for example in The High Road. Each step now fades into the next. Fast movement can show some ghosting. On Direct3D 11 slow motion still shows the steps.

## More copies accepted

- Copies of the **USA** and **Europe (French, German)** discs with the media check patch are now recognised, as the English European disc's already was. This one-byte difference does not change how the game runs.

## Getting started

New to the port? See the [v1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/007eon-recomp/releases/tag/v1.0.0). No game files are included: you need your own copy of the game.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
