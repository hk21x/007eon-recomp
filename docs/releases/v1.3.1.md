An update to the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox, Europe and USA).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.1.0 or 1.2.0:** open `EoNLauncher.exe` and press **Update** at the top of its window. Your saves and settings
are kept. **From 1.0.x:** the launcher offers this release the same way; press **Update the launcher** first, then
**Update**.

## What's new since 1.2.0

### Driving missions above 60 frames a second (experimental)
- New option **High frame rate (experimental)** (F1, off by default). With a frame rate cap above 60 (Frame rate cap,
  F1), the driving missions show extra frames between the game's own 60 a second, so motion is smoother on a high
  refresh rate monitor: at a cap of 144, up to 120 frames a second.
- The game itself still runs at 60, so its speed and handling are unchanged. The picture runs up to one game step
  (about 17 ms) behind.
- Driving missions only. On foot stays at 60: the game is built around a fixed 60, and the extra frames are too costly
  there for now.
- Reflections and the floor's lighting in the driving missions move at 60 while the rest of the picture is smoother.

### Driving missions: the mouse moves the camera
- In a car, moving the mouse swings the camera round the car; it eases back behind the car a second after the mouse
  stops. Looking back works as before.
- On foot in a driving mission (Train Chase), the mouse turns the camera, as the right stick does.
- Mouse up looks up, as on foot; **Invert mouse** (F1) flips it.

### Renderer
- Drawing is now spread over more CPU cores (shared with the other ports on the same toolkit), which leaves more time
  for each frame.

### Smaller changes
- A mission's intro movie can be skipped with **A** the first time too, not only once you have seen it.
- With Free aim off, the lock-on's red dot now moves the same way as the camera for the mouse's up and down, so one
  **Invert mouse** setting fits both.
- Menus: a controller that Windows reports twice no longer moves two rows for each press.

### Fixes
- The game's **Subtitles** option is now kept from one start to the next (it went back to Off at every start, and a
  loaded save or the language could change it). It is also in F1 as **Subtitles**.
- Movie subtitles ran twice as fast as the speech; they now keep the movie's time.
- With the keyboard and mouse, the view swung round Bond by itself at the start of a mission (An Old Friend most of
  all), and the game read the sticks as pushed until the first key (the menus moving at start).
- Train Chase on foot: **W and S** now walk Bond with a controller plugged in too (they did nothing while one was
  connected).

## Files

- **EoNLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **EoN-PAL-package.zip**, **EoN-USA-package.zip** and their **.json**: the packages the launcher downloads, and their
  sizes and checksums. You do not need to download or unzip them.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
