An update to the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox, Europe and USA).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.1.0 or later:** open `EoNLauncher.exe` and press **Update** at the top of its window. Your saves and settings
are kept. **From 1.0.x:** the launcher offers this release the same way; press **Update the launcher** first, then
**Update**.

## What's new since 1.3.1

### Free aim on the rope
- Free aim now works while Bond rappels (A Long Way Down and the other ropes). Before, the mouse only nudged the view
  and there was no crosshair, so shooting needed the lock-on.
- The mouse moves the view directly and it stays where you put it (up to 60 degrees to each side, 30 up and down). The
  view sits over Bond's shoulder, the crosshair is drawn, the left button's shots go to it and the right button zooms,
  as on foot.
- The camera's tilt on the rope is left out while the mouse aims, so mouse up is up on the screen. With the lock-on
  held, or with a controller, the camera is the game's own.
- Enemies level with Bond far to the side are outside the view's reach; the lock-on still gets those.

### Fixes
- With Free aim on, a shot through a sniper scope (the Dragunov) landed up and to the right of the scope's crosshair,
  further off the more it was zoomed. Scoped shots now go where the crosshair is.
- The scope's picture no longer jumps about a second after it opens.

## Files

- **EoNLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **EoN-PAL-package.zip**, **EoN-USA-package.zip** and their **.json**: the packages the launcher downloads, and their
  sizes and checksums. You do not need to download or unzip them.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
