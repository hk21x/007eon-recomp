An update to the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox, Europe and USA), above all
for playing with a keyboard and mouse.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

**From 1.1.0:** open `EoNLauncher.exe` and press **Update** at the top of its window. Your saves and settings are
kept. **From 1.0.x:** the launcher offers this release the same way; press **Update the launcher** first, then
**Update**.

## What's new since 1.1.0

### Mouse look and free aim (on foot)
- The mouse turns the camera directly, like a PC third-person shooter: no drift after you stop, no swinging behind
  Bond unless you want it (**Camera follows movement**, PC Settings, Mouse 2).
- **Free aim** (on by default with the keyboard and mouse; F1 turns it off): hold the **right button** to aim over
  Bond's shoulder at a crosshair in the middle of the view, and the **left button** to fire. Shots go where the
  crosshair is, also from the hip (a fainter crosshair shows when firing without aiming).
- While aiming, **A, D and S strafe** and step back, as when locked on, with the gun kept on the aim.
- **Hit markers** on the crosshair: white for a hit, red for a kill, wider for a head shot.
- **Wall cover:** at the edge of a cover, the right button leans Bond out to aim with the mouse; let go to go back.
- **Snipers:** the right button opens the scope and letting go closes it; the **wheel** zooms. The wheel also zooms
  a mounted gun, which the mouse now aims directly.
- **Lock on** is now the **middle button** (hold).
- New options on PC Settings' **Mouse 2** page: **Aim sensitivity** (the lock and scopes), **Sensitivity while
  aiming**, **Aim height** (how high the crosshair sits).
- **Mouse up now looks up on the normal camera**, as it does when aiming and in scopes. If you had turned on Invert
  mouse (or the game's Invert Vertical) to make the camera feel right, turn it off again.

### Keys
- **R** reloads, with Bond's animation; **C** crouches; **F** is wall cover.
- **1 to 6** pick a weapon, in the order the next weapon key goes through them; **X** swaps back to the last one.
  The **mouse wheel** also changes weapon. The arrow keys keep the gadgets.
- **Backspace** goes back in the menus.
- The **Field of view** setting now works on foot (it did nothing before).

### Menus
- The mouse shows a pointer in the menus: the item under it is highlighted and a click selects it; a click on a
  value's arrows changes it. The prompts along the bottom show the mouse buttons for Select and Back.

### Driving missions
- The **mouse wheel** changes weapon.
- The mouse aims the **tank's, rail tank's and helicopter's** gun. On the rail tank (A Show of Force) the left
  button fires and the right aims.
- The helicopter (Sand Storm) climbs on **E** and descends on **Q**.
- On foot in a driving mission (Train Chase), **W and S** walk Bond.

### Fixes
- A crash when starting or restarting a mission (after playing another).

## Files

- **EoNLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **EoN-PAL-package.zip**, **EoN-USA-package.zip** and their **.json**: the packages the launcher downloads, and their
  sizes and checksums. You do not need to download or unzip them.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
