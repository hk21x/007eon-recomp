An update of the unofficial native PC port of 007: Everything or Nothing (original Xbox).

**Download this launcher once by hand.** From this version on, the launcher tells you when a new version is out and
updates itself, then offers to rebuild the game with it. Your saves and settings are kept.

## New: every retail disc

The port now works with all three retail releases:

- **Europe (English, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Swedish)** and **Europe (French, German)**: the same game code as
  before, now recognised from an unmodified disc. The French/German disc plays in French (it has no English).
- **USA (NTSC)**: a separate build of the game with every part of the port adapted to it. The launcher recognises
  your disc and builds the right one.

## Fixes

- **Crashes in the driving missions.** Two crashes, one a couple of seconds into loading a driving mission and one
  partway through the mission after Death's Door, came from parts of the game that were safe on the Xbox's single
  processor but not on a PC's several cores. Both are fixed, in the driving missions and on foot.
- **Microsoft Defender warning.** Defender could flag `EoN.exe` (Behavior:Win32/DefenseEvasion.A!ml) when the game
  switched between the on-foot and driving missions, because of how the next part was started. It now starts the
  ordinary way. If Defender quarantined your copy, restore it in Windows Security (Protection history), then update.

## Launcher

- Tells you when a new version is out (**Update the launcher**, **What's new**, **Later**), downloads it, checks it and
  restarts; then offers to rebuild the game with that version.
- Builds the released version of the game, not work in progress.

## Getting started

New to the port? See the [v1.0.0 notes](https://github.com/hk21x/007eon-recomp/releases/tag/v1.0.0). No game files are
included: you need your own copy of the game.

Problems: the game's log is in `EoN\play\logs`, the launcher's in `EoN\launcher.log`.
